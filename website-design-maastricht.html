FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a website cost in Maastricht?

It depends entirely on scope — a simple one-page site costs far less than a full Shopify store with custom features. We quote every project individually once we understand what you need, rather than starting from a fixed package.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects take a few weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how much content and how many custom features are involved.

Do you build in English and Dutch?

Yes — we build and maintain sites bilingually, which matters in an international city like Maastricht where customers search in both languages.

Can you help me set up a Shopify store specifically?

Yes, Shopify store setup, product catalog structure, and payment/checkout configuration are one of our core services.

What happens after my site launches?

We offer ongoing growth and maintenance plans covering updates, fixes, SEO, and Google Business Profile management, so your site keeps working for you after launch, not just on day one.
