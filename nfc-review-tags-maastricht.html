Most happy customers never leave a review, simply because asking them to open an app, find your business, and type it out is too much friction. An NFC review tag removes almost all of that friction: a customer taps their phone against a small tag or card, and their browser opens straight to your Google review page, ready to write.

We design the tag to match your branding, link it directly to your Google Business Profile, and hand it to you ready to place at your checkout counter, reception desk, or table. No subscription, no app for you or your customers to install — it's a physical object that does one job well.

It pairs naturally with a stronger online presence overall: more recent, higher-volume reviews are one of the biggest factors in how well your business ranks in local Google search and Maps results.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is an NFC review tag?

A small NFC-enabled tag or card that customers tap with their phone to go straight to your Google review page — no app, no QR code, no typing required.

Do customers need an app to use it?

No. Most smartphones from 2018 onward have NFC built in, so tapping the tag opens your review page directly in the browser.

Where should I place the tag?

Most businesses place it at the checkout counter, on a table stand, or at the reception desk — anywhere a customer naturally pauses after a good experience.

How long does an NFC tag last?

NFC chips are rated for tens of thousands of taps, so a single durable card typically lasts years of daily counter use.

Can I combine this with my website?

Yes — we link it directly to your Google Business Profile and can pair it with review-request follow-ups from your website or contact form.
