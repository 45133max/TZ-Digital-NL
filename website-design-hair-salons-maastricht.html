Website Design for Salons

A Salon Website Built to Fill Your Chair, Not Just Look Nice

Custom websites for hair salons and barbershops in Maastricht, built around online booking, your best work, and local search.

Get a quote

A salon website has one job: turn someone scrolling on their phone into a booked appointment. That means fast load times, a gallery that actually shows off your cuts and color work, and a clear path to book or call — not a generic template with a stock photo of scissors.

We've built and maintain the websites for de Vriendschap Kapsalon and de Vriendschap, two hair and beauty salons here in Maastricht, so we know what actually matters for a salon site: booking, galleries, and being found by people searching nearby.

Maastricht is a genuinely international city, so we build and maintain salon sites bilingually in English and Dutch by default — your English-speaking students and expat clients search differently than your Dutch regulars, and the site should work for both.

FAQ

Common Questions

Can you add online booking to a salon website?

Yes — booking system integration is available on request, so clients can book an appointment directly from your site instead of calling or messaging.

Can I showcase my work, like before/after photos?

Yes, we build in a gallery or portfolio section designed for showing off cuts, color work, and treatments, since that's usually what convinces a new client to book.

Do you have experience with salon websites specifically?

Yes — we've built and maintain the websites for de Vriendschap Kapsalon and de Vriendschap, both hair and beauty salons here in Maastricht.

Can this connect to Instagram and Google reviews?

Yes — we can sync website and Instagram content, and pair your site with an NFC review tag at the counter to turn happy walk-ins into 5-star Google reviews.

Do you build in English and Dutch?

Yes — we build and maintain sites bilingually by default, which matters for a salon in an international city like Maastricht.

Once your site is live, pair it with local SEO for hair salons to get found by people searching for a salon near them.
