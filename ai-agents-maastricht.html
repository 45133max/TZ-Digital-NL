Every missed message is a customer who might just go to the next business instead. An AI agent sits on your website (or WhatsApp) and answers the questions you get asked constantly — opening hours, prices, availability — instantly, at 11pm on a Sunday just as well as at 11am on a Tuesday.

FAQ

Common Questions

What can an AI agent actually do for my business?

It answers common customer questions (opening hours, prices, services) and can handle bookings, any time of day, without needing a person to respond.

Will it sound robotic?

No — we train it on your business's actual tone, services, and information, so it sounds like a knowledgeable member of your team, not a generic chatbot.

Does it work with WhatsApp or just my website?

Both are possible — we scope this based on where your customers actually reach out to you.

What if it can't answer a question?

It's set up to hand off to you directly, by email or notification, rather than guess, so nothing important falls through the cracks.

How long does it take to set up?

Timeline depends on scope, but most AI agents are trained and live within a couple of weeks — get in touch for a quote based on what you need.
