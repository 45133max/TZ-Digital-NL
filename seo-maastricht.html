Your Google Business Profile carries more weight in local search rankings than almost anything on your website itself — category selection, review volume and recency, and profile completeness are the biggest single factor. We set it up properly and keep it active, rather than treating it as a one-time checkbox.

On top of that, we handle the on-page and technical side: page titles and descriptions that actually target what customers search for, content depth, site speed, and consistent business information across the web (directories, listings, your own site) so Google trusts what it finds.

Maastricht searches happen in both English and Dutch — most agencies only optimize for one. We build and optimize for both by default, which is a real gap in this specific market.

FAQ

Common Questions

What does local SEO actually involve?

A mix of Google Business Profile optimization, on-page content and technical fixes on your website, consistent business information across directories, and building genuine local reviews and backlinks.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Local SEO typically takes a few months to show meaningful movement — it's a compounding effort, not an overnight switch, and we're upfront about that timeline rather than overpromising.

Do you optimize for English and Dutch search?

Yes — Maastricht is genuinely bilingual, so we optimize content and structure for both English and Dutch search queries, not just one.

Do I need a new website for SEO to work?

Not necessarily — we can often improve an existing site's SEO without a full rebuild, though a badly-structured site sometimes needs foundational fixes first.

What's included in an SEO package?

It depends on your starting point — could include Google Business Profile setup and management, on-page content optimization, technical fixes, and ongoing reporting. We scope it after reviewing your current presence.
